# Account Lockout and Unlock
Category: Access

## Overview
Accounts lock automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock by themselves after 30 minutes. A locked account blocks VPN, email and application access.

## Unlock an account
1. Wait 30 minutes for the automatic unlock, or use the self-service unlock page and verify with MFA.
2. The helpdesk automation can unlock the account after the directory confirms the account is locked.
3. After unlocking, sign in again with the correct password.

## Preventing repeat lockouts
Old saved passwords on a phone, tablet, mail client or mapped drive are the most common cause of repeated lockouts. Update the saved password on every device after a password change.
