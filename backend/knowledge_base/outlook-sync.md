# Outlook Synchronization Troubleshooting
Category: Email

## Overview
Outlook synchronization problems show as email not updating, items stuck in the Outbox, or a Disconnected or Trying to connect message in the status bar. Causes include being offline, a damaged local cache, a full mailbox or an outdated Office version.

## Fix Outlook not synchronizing
1. Check the status bar and make sure Work Offline is turned off on the Send/Receive tab.
2. Confirm your internet or VPN connection works and open webmail to check the mailbox itself.
3. Close and restart Outlook, then press Send/Receive All Folders.
4. Clear the Outlook cache: close Outlook, rename the local OST cache file and reopen Outlook so it rebuilds the cache. The helpdesk automation can clear the cache for you.
5. Check that the mailbox is not full and update Microsoft Office to the latest version.
6. If synchronization still fails, raise an incident for Messaging Support.
