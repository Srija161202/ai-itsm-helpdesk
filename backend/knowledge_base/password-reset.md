# Password Reset
Category: Access

## Overview
Corporate passwords expire every 90 days. A password must be at least 12 characters and cannot reuse your last 10 passwords. Expired passwords block sign-in to the laptop, email, VPN and business applications.

## Reset an expired or forgotten password
1. Open the company sign-in page and choose Forgot or expired password.
2. Verify your identity with the MFA prompt sent to your registered mobile device.
3. Set a new password that meets the policy and sign in again.
The helpdesk automation can also reset an expired password after the directory confirms it has expired. A temporary credential is sent to your registered mobile and must be changed at next sign-in.

## After the reset
Connect to the office network or VPN so the laptop syncs the new password, then sign out of Outlook, Teams and mobile email and sign back in with the new password to avoid repeated account lockouts.
